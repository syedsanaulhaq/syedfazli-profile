Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Syed Fazli (trading as Hosting Ocean Solutions) ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the HO Weather Map mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Precise Location Data

To provide the core functionality of the App (showing your current position on the weather and hazard map), the App requires access to your device's precise location (GPS and network-based).

  • Purpose: This data is used to display live weather conditions, flood reports, and route intelligence relative to your current position.
  • Usage: Location data is processed while the app is in use.

B. Personal Information

If you choose to register an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Country/Region

C. Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect certain information about your mobile device, including:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • App usage statistics and performance logs (via Capacitor and standard Android logs).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and maintain the live weather map.
  • Allow you to participate in the Traveler Community features.
  • Improve App performance and fix technical issues.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or App updates.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information or location data to third parties. We only share data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may use third-party tools (like Google Play Services or Firebase) to help the app function. These providers have access to information only to perform tasks on our behalf.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be 100% secure.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Location Access: You can enable or disable location permissions at any time through your Android device settings. Note that disabling location will prevent the weather map from showing data for your current area.
  • Account Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us at the email provided below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
